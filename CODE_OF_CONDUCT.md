# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Build4Andhra is a learning and building community. We want people with different skills, backgrounds, languages, locations, and experience levels to feel welcome to participate, ask questions, teach, and collaborate.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, patient, and inclusive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Help newcomers learn; do not shame people for questions or mistakes.
- Give credit and respect authorship, licenses, and attribution.
- Be honest about evidence, limitations, conflicts of interest, and AI assistance.
- Respect accessibility needs, language differences, and varied levels of experience.
- Protect private information and use responsible disclosure for security issues.
- Follow applicable law, GitHub policies, and project-specific rules.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, doxxing, sexual harassment, hate speech, deliberate intimidation, repeated personal attacks, impersonation, retaliation against good-faith reports, spam, malicious disruption, unauthorized access, and publication of private or confidential data are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, events, official community channels, and conduct when someone represents Build4Andhra.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the designated maintainers through the contact method published on the organization profile or repository. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly. If a report concerns a maintainer, report it to another maintainer or the platform.

Maintainers will assess reports fairly and keep details confidential as far as reasonably possible. Responses may include a warning, content moderation, temporary participation limits, removal from a role, or exclusion from community spaces. Serious or repeated misconduct may result in immediate action. Conflicts of interest should be disclosed and affected decision-makers should recuse themselves where appropriate.

GitHub's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines continue to apply. This code does not replace applicable law or emergency services.
