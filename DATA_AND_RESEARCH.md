# Data, Research, and Responsible Technology Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing a dataset, model, research result, tutorial using real data, or public-facing application.

## Provenance and permissions

- [ ] Source and collection method documented
- [ ] License and terms checked for intended use
- [ ] Permission obtained where required
- [ ] No confidential, restricted, or unlawfully obtained material
- [ ] Attribution and required notices included

## Privacy and ethics

- [ ] Personal-data necessity and applicable processing requirements assessed
- [ ] Appropriate notice, consent, or other legal basis identified
- [ ] Data minimization, retention, access control, and deletion planned
- [ ] Human-subject or institutional review completed where applicable
- [ ] Risks involving children and vulnerable people reviewed
- [ ] Re-identification and linkage risks assessed

## AI and model safety

- [ ] Model, weights, code, and training/evaluation data licenses checked separately
- [ ] Limitations, evaluation method, and known biases documented
- [ ] High-impact use cases reviewed by qualified people
- [ ] Human oversight, appeal, and fallback paths considered
- [ ] Security and abuse cases assessed
- [ ] Claims match the available evidence

## Learning resources and events

- [ ] Sources and author credits included
- [ ] Third-party material is licensed or used with permission
- [ ] Accessibility and language needs considered
- [ ] Recording, photography, and publication permissions obtained where needed
- [ ] No unsupported promises about jobs, certificates, academic credit, or outcomes

## Public-sector projects

- [ ] Written authorization for system and data access
- [ ] Data-sharing agreements and approvals obtained where required
- [ ] Hosting, security, audit, and retention conditions checked
- [ ] No implication of government endorsement without written authorization

A checklist does not replace legal, security, or ethics review.
