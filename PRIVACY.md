# Privacy and Data Handling

Build4Andhra aims to collect as little personal data as practical. GitHub processes account and activity data under its own terms and privacy policy; this repository does not control the platform's processing.

## Community participation

Do not post phone numbers, home addresses, government identifiers, personal records, credentials, private messages, or sensitive data in public issues, commits, logs, datasets, screenshots, or discussions. Use private reporting for sensitive concerns.

## Projects that process personal data

Before a project collects or processes personal data, document:
- purpose and necessity;
- data source and applicable lawful basis, notice, consent, or other requirement;
- user rights, retention, deletion, and security controls;
- access roles, processors, hosting, and transfers;
- risks to children and vulnerable people; and
- legal, institutional, and contractual approvals.

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 include commencement and phased implementation provisions. Check which provisions are in force for the relevant processing at launch and seek specialist advice where needed. Other laws may apply in parallel.

Use synthetic or properly anonymized data for demos and tests when possible. Pseudonymization alone does not necessarily anonymize data.

## Learning, events, and research

Projects involving human participants, students, children, health, or institutional records require additional scrutiny. Obtain institutional ethics review, consent, safeguarding, and permissions where applicable. A learning or public-interest purpose does not itself authorize data collection or publication.

## Incidents

Report suspected exposure promptly through the private security/contact channel. Restrict access, preserve only what is needed for investigation, and follow applicable notification and response obligations.
