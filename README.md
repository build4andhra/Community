# Build4Andhra

**Learn together. Build together. Grow together.**

Build4Andhra is a community-led, open-source learning and innovation ecosystem. We bring people together to upskill, share knowledge, mentor one another, collaborate across disciplines, and build useful projects in public.

Andhra Pradesh is part of our identity and starting point—not a limit on who can participate or what we can build. Contributors from anywhere are welcome.

## What we do

- **Learn and upskill:** study groups, learning paths, peer learning, workshops, talks, and mentoring.
- **Build in public:** open-source projects, prototypes, developer tools, portfolios, and collaborative experiments.
- **Share knowledge:** tutorials, notes, demos, technical writing, translations, and reusable learning resources.
- **Research and explore:** AI, software engineering, cybersecurity, data, design, cloud, hardware, and interdisciplinary topics.
- **Connect people:** students, self-learners, developers, educators, researchers, professionals, startups, and institutions.
- **Create opportunities:** project teams, contribution pathways, community events, and constructive feedback.

## Who can participate?

Anyone who wants to learn, teach, build, or contribute respectfully—regardless of experience level, employer, institution, or location. No particular degree, job title, or prior open-source experience is required.

## Start here

1. Read the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
2. See [how to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md).
3. Explore the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and open issues.
4. Suggest a project, learning session, study group, tutorial, or collaboration.
5. Share what you learn and help the next person get started.

## What we value

Curiosity, practical learning, generosity, transparency, accessibility, responsible technology, respectful disagreement, good documentation, and sustainable maintenance.

## Independence and representation

Build4Andhra is an independent, community-led initiative unless a formal relationship is explicitly documented. Participation does not imply endorsement by a government department, university, employer, company, or other institution. Do not claim authority to represent an institution or sign agreements on its behalf.

## Licensing

Each repository must clearly declare its own license. A public GitHub repository is not automatically open source. See [Licensing and Intellectual Property](LICENSING.md). Software, documentation, datasets, model weights, third-party assets, and branding may require different terms.

## Responsible participation

Do not publish secrets, credentials, private or confidential information, personal data, restricted records, or material you lack permission to share. Do not test systems without explicit authorization. Use the private security reporting process described in [SECURITY.md].
