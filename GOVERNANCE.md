# Governance

## 1. Status and purpose

Build4Andhra is an independent, community-led initiative focused on learning, upskilling, open-source building, knowledge sharing, mentorship, research, and collaboration. Unless separately and validly established, it is not a registered legal entity and cannot claim the legal status, tax benefits, authority, or endorsements of a society, trust, Section 8 company, government body, university, or other institution.

GitHub organization ownership and repository permissions are platform roles; they do not automatically make a person a legal representative of a separate entity.

## 2. Principles

- Open participation and learning by doing
- Publicly documented decisions where practical
- Respectful feedback and shared credit
- Merit-based review and sustainable maintenance
- Respect for contributor rights and project licenses
- Privacy, security, accessibility, and responsible data use
- Disclosure of relevant conflicts of interest

## 3. Roles

**Participant:** joins events, discussions, study groups, or learning activities.

**Contributor:** contributes code, documentation, teaching, research, design, testing, or community work.

**Reviewer:** provides technical, educational, or policy review.

**Maintainer:** reviews and merges changes, manages releases, and applies repository policies.

**Organization owner:** manages organization-level settings and access. Owner privileges should be limited to trusted people and protected with strong authentication.

Roles reflect responsibilities, not personal status. Grant only the access needed. Establish recovery procedures and, when feasible, at least two trusted owners.

## 4. Decisions

Routine changes may be accepted by maintainers through pull-request review. Significant changes—licensing, governance, major partnerships, funds, repository transfers, or identity—should be proposed publicly with reasons and a reasonable comment period.

Seek consensus. If consensus is not possible, document the decision, rationale, alternatives, and material dissent. Disclose conflicts of interest and recuse where appropriate.

Urgent action to protect people, credentials, systems, or legal rights may be taken immediately; document the action afterwards when safe.

## 5. Learning spaces and events

Facilitators should set clear goals, accessibility expectations, and participation rules. Do not promise jobs, certificates, academic credit, funding, or endorsements unless authorized and confirmed. Respect participants' privacy and obtain permission before publishing photos, recordings, or personal details.

## 6. Partnerships and public representation

No participant may promise funding, sign contracts, speak for a government department, bind an employer or university, or represent Build4Andhra as a registered legal entity without documented authority. Disclose sponsorships, gifts, employer relationships, and conflicts. Institutional partnerships require written confirmation and relevant approvals.

## 7. Funds

Do not collect donations, sponsorship money, membership fees, or event payments in the initiative's name until a responsible legal and financial arrangement, accounting process, and authorized signatories are established. Keep appropriate records and disclose material conflicts.

## 8. Amendments

Propose governance changes through an issue and pull request. Explain the impact, allow community feedback, and record the accepted version and effective date.
