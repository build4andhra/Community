# Licensing and Intellectual Property

## Each repository must declare its license

A public GitHub repository is not automatically open source. Each repository must include a license that clearly states the permissions granted. Do not assume a license in one repository applies to another.

## Suggested defaults

- **Software:** MIT for simple, permissive reuse; Apache-2.0 when explicit patent-license terms are useful; GPL-3.0 when the project's goals call for copyleft obligations.
- **Documentation and learning materials:** CC BY 4.0 may suit original tutorials, notes, diagrams, and presentations.
- **Datasets:** choose terms only after checking provenance, privacy, database rights, consent, contracts, and redistribution permissions.
- **Fonts, images, maps, models, and other assets:** track each source and license separately. Review model weights, training data, and usage restrictions individually.
- **Name and logo:** not automatically covered by a software license. See `BRANDING.md`.

## License scope

A license applies only to material owned by the relevant rights holders and covered by it. It does not grant rights to third-party content, trademarks, government emblems, personal data, or material with unclear ownership.

## Contributions

Contributors retain copyright unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. By submitting a contribution, contributors agree it may be distributed under the repository's stated license, provided they have the necessary rights. Do not add a CLA or DCO retroactively without appropriate review and permissions.

## Third-party notices

Maintain `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` where dependencies or copied assets require attribution or notices. Review dependency licenses before distribution and preserve required notices.

## Government and public data

Public availability is not blanket permission to reuse material. Check the specific source policy, license, terms, classification, privacy requirements, and attribution requirements. Do not use official seals or logos to imply endorsement.

For unclear ownership, do not publish until permission or legal review resolves the issue.
