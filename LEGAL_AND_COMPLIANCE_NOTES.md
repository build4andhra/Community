# Legal and Compliance Notes — India and Andhra Pradesh

**Operational checklist, not a legal opinion or certification.** This is an initial guide for an early-stage learning and open-source community, not an exhaustive legal audit. Verify current consolidated legislation, rules, commencement notifications, government orders, and project-specific facts with qualified Indian counsel before relying on it.

## 1. Open-source licensing and copyright

The Copyright Act, 1957, applicable rules, contracts, and third-party license terms may apply. Public availability does not remove copyright. Add an explicit license, preserve required notices, review dependency/model/font/map/image/dataset licenses, and do not accept work the contributor lacks rights to submit.

India Code: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/1367

## 2. Information technology and cyber security

The Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable rules may apply to digital services, unauthorized access, security incidents, and certain data or platform activities. Review current text and applicable CERT-In directions for covered entities and incidents. Community policies do not authorize security testing of third-party systems.

India Code: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/1999

## 3. Personal data

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 contain commencement and phased implementation provisions. Check the specific provisions in force for the processing activity at the time. Assess roles, purpose, notices, consent or other applicable requirements, rights, security, retention, processors, and incident duties. Other laws may apply in parallel.

Act: https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf  
Rules explanatory note: https://www.meity.gov.in/writereaddata/files/Explanatory-Note-DPDP-Rules-2025.pdf

## 4. Online services and intermediary obligations

The Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, as amended, may be relevant depending on the service and facts. Hosting code on GitHub does not by itself establish that Build4Andhra is an intermediary or that every intermediary obligation applies. Seek advice before operating a separate user-content platform or service.

MeitY: https://www.meity.gov.in/

## 5. Formal registration in Andhra Pradesh

The Andhra Pradesh Societies Registration Act, 2001 provides a framework for qualifying societies. Whether registration is appropriate depends on actual objectives, membership, governing documents, activities, and funding. A GitHub organization alone is not a registered society.

Act: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15650/1/ap_societies_registration_act%2C_2001.pdf

Before formalizing, seek advice on registration, governing documents, accounts, filings, tax treatment, and local requirements. Charitable tax benefits, CSR eligibility, public grants, and foreign contributions may involve additional rules. Do not accept foreign contribution without specialist review and required permissions.

## 6. Government data, maps, and official identity

Public availability is not blanket permission to republish. Check source terms, license, data-sharing agreements, classification, confidentiality, privacy, and attribution. Government systems require explicit authorization for testing or integration. Do not use official seals or departmental logos to imply endorsement without authorization.

## 7. Education, research, and events

Projects involving human participants, students, children, health, or institutional records may require ethics review, consent, institutional permission, and safeguarding. Events may require venue, safety, photography, accessibility, or other permissions depending on circumstances. Do not promise certificates, academic credit, jobs, or outcomes without confirmed authority.

## 8. Employment and institutional IP

Contributors should confirm they have rights to contribute code, designs, data, and inventions. Employer contracts, university IP policies, confidentiality duties, government service rules, procurement terms, and grant conditions may affect contributions. Do not use restricted data or work product without permission.

## 9. Funds, sponsorships, and partnerships

Before collecting fees or donations, signing contracts, issuing receipts, paying vendors, or promising deliverables, establish who is legally responsible and authorized. Keep appropriate records and disclose conflicts. Tax, consumer, event, venue, and local permissions may apply.

## 10. Project launch checklist

- [ ] Repository license and third-party notices checked
- [ ] Maintainer and contribution rights identified
- [ ] Data sources, permissions, and privacy risks documented
- [ ] Security review and private reporting path set
- [ ] AI/model risks and claims reviewed where applicable
- [ ] Required institutional or government approvals obtained
- [ ] Event and publication permissions checked where applicable
- [ ] Funding and public representations authorized
- [ ] Current law and commencement notifications checked
- [ ] Specialist review obtained for high-risk or regulated activities

## 11. Review cadence

Review these notes at least every six months and whenever the community begins processing personal data, handling money, partnering with institutions, hosting events, or deploying public services. Record the review date and sources consulted.

These notes do not certify compliance with every applicable law.
